# Price-Change Pilot — Day-15 Decision Memo

**As of 2026-05-21 · 14 days pre / 14 days post · 346 price-↑ SKUs · 6 pilot stores · 6 paired non-pilot controls · ~195 other-chain stores**

---

## Verdict

**Do not roll out chain-wide yet. Extend the pilot another 30 days with two interventions. Make the final go/no-go at day 45.**

The price hike achieved its primary commercial goal — **gross-margin % expanded by +7.5 pp DiD vs paired non-pilot** — without measurable patient churn. The cost is a ~5% volume drop on price-↑ SKUs and a small but clinically concerning lapse on chronic-medicine refills. The volume picture is too early to call: most chronic-refill cycles are 30 days, and habit-change signals (repeat / churn) need 60 days. A 30-day extension de-risks the full rollout for negligible cost.

## What's working

- **Margin expansion is real.** +7.5 pp DiD on GM% is exactly what the hike was for, and it's holding consistently across pilot stores.
- **No patient flight.** Patient count flat (−0.2 pp DiD). Repeat-patient count actually up (+1.2 pp DiD). Walk-aways at 0.55%.
- **Acceptance is quiet.** Customers are largely paying the higher price without resistance. Zero GENRTN coupon uses suggests staff aren't being asked for the price match.

## What needs more time

- **Volume drop (~5%) could compound.** AOV is down 5.4% DiD — customers are dropping items per visit. Whether this stabilises or worsens over the next refill cycle is the open question.
- **Chronic-refill lapse cohort.** Of the 4 partial-bill patients who fully stopped buying their pre-hike price-↑ SKUs, **3 were on chronic meds** (BP, diabetes, cholesterol). Absolute count is small but the clinical implication is severe — stopping these meds is not just lost LTV, it's a health-outcome risk.
- **All-Other context.** ~80% of pilot's revenue drop is mirrored in the rest of the chain (non-pilot/non-paired stores down −4.2% on the same SKUs). Suggests **some of the "price effect" is actually a chain-wide May seasonality**. The true DiD on volume may be smaller than the headline.
- **Maturity.** 15 days is too short for 30-day refill cycles and 60-day churn signals to land.

## Recommended interventions during the 30-day extension

1. **Activate GENRTN price-match on the chronic-lapse cohort now.** Outbound call to the 4 fully-lapsed patients (BP/diabetes/cholesterol) with the price-match offer. Measure win-back rate. This both protects clinical continuity AND tests whether GENRTN is a viable safety net before chain rollout.
2. **Weekly chronic-lapse tracking.** Re-run the partial-bill cohort analysis weekly. If chronic-lapse spreads beyond 5% of price-↑ chronic patients over the next 30 days, that's a stop-rollout signal regardless of GM%.

## Decision checkpoint — Day 45 (2026-06-20)

**Roll out chain-wide if all three hold:**
- GM% DiD ≥ **+5 pp** vs paired non-pilot
- Chronic-refill lapse rate ≤ **5%** of price-↑ chronic patients in pilot
- Patient count DiD ≥ **−2%** vs paired non-pilot

**Partial rollout (city-by-city) if GM% gain holds but chronic-lapse approaches 5%.**

**Roll back the hike on chronic SKUs only** (keep on acute / therapy-cycle) if chronic-lapse breaches 5% or GM% gain compresses below +3 pp.
